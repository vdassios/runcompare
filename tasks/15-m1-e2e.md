# 15 · M1 end-to-end

**Milestone** M1 · **Depends on** 09, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 · **Branch** `task/15-m1-e2e`

## Goal
Prove M1 works end to end with real files through the real UI, and fix the integration issues that only appear once all the pieces meet.

## Read first
`tasks/README.md`, all of `PLAN.md`, and the PR descriptions of tasks 09–14 for any open questions they left.

## You own
`tests/e2e/m1.spec.ts` and `README.md` (usage section and a screenshot). You may also make **small integration fixes in any file**, each listed in the PR with the reason. Anything larger becomes a follow-up note in the PR, not a fix.

## Requirements
Playwright e2e on the normal build (no `?fixture`), using the input's `setInputFiles`:
1. Upload `fixtures/trail-2024.fit`, then `trail-2025.fit`. 2024 becomes the baseline.
2. The KPI Time card shows `+9:05` (±0:03) for 2025.
3. The splits totals row shows the same total delta, and 55 rows in metric mode.
4. Switching units to imperial gives 34 rows, and distances are shown in mi.
5. Hovering the middle of the delta chart shows the map's hover marker. Hovering the map line shows the chart tooltip.
6. Dragging the zoom slider narrows the visible range on every chart. Check this through `zoomRange` in the store.
7. Setting 2025 as the baseline turns 2024's delta into `−9:05`.
8. Removing 2025 (with the inline confirm) leaves one attempt and the delta chart hides.
9. Upload the two GPX heads in a fresh page: the walking card shows "estimated" and there is no cadence chart.
10. Upload a `.txt` file: an error alert appears and can be dismissed.

Log the time from upload until `ready` in the PR.

Replace the README's screenshot with a full-page capture of the `fit-two-attempts` real scenario in light mode.

## Acceptance
- `npm run test:e2e` passes locally and in CI.
- Every integration fix is listed, and no test was weakened to pass.
- A **manual check** in Chrome by the agent, if it has a browser tool, or else a checklist for the owner in the PR covering:
  - hover sync
  - zoom
  - the theme switch
  - the mobile layout at 390 px with no horizontal scroll

## Out of scope
M2 and M3 features.
