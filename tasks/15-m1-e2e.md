Steps:
1. Upload `fixtures/trail-2024.fit`, then `trail-2025.fit`. 2024 becomes the baseline.
2. The KPI Time card shows `+9:05` (±0:03) for 2025.
3. The splits totals row shows the ghost delta at the finish, `+6:23` (±0:03), and 55 rows in metric mode. It differs from the Time card by the 163 s spent standing at the 2025 finish.
4. Switching units to imperial gives 34 rows, and distances are shown in mi.
5. Setting 2025 as the baseline turns 2024's Time card delta into `−9:05`.
6. Removing 2025 (with the inline confirm) leaves one attempt and the delta chart hides.
7. Upload the two GPX heads into a fresh store: the walking card shows "estimated" and there is no cadence chart.
8. Upload a `.txt` file: an error alert appears and can be dismissed.
